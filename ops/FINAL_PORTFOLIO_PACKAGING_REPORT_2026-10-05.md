## 5. Final product positioning

The MVP should be described as:

> a humanities citation-verification / evidence-retrieval assistant that narrows large primary sources to plausible, traceable passages and returns original-page evidence for human review.

It should NOT be described as:

- automatic citation truth verification;
- guaranteed Top-1 correctness;
- exhaustive literature retrieval;
- automatic scholarly interpretation;
- a production-scale hosted service.
